About AI Verse (short, 40 words)

AI Verse is a California AI company that makes free AI tools for everyday life and AIV X, an AI platform that builds AI assistants and AI apps from a plain-language description. Its slogan is "Build Your AI Future." Learn more at aiverse.it.com.

About AI Verse (long, 110 words)

AI Verse is a California AI company building practical AI for everyday life. Its seven free AI apps help people study, plan their day, learn about money and markets, train and eat well, edit photos and find jobs, each with 20 free AI uses a day. Ask AIV is a free AI assistant, and AI Business lets anyone create a personal AI assistant without code. AI Verse's AI platform, AIV X, builds full AI assistants and AI apps from a description, then trains and deploys them. For businesses, AI Verse builds AI chatbots and custom AI tools, and developers can use the OpenAI-compatible AIV API. Learn more at aiverse.it.com.

About AIV X

AIV X is the AI platform from AI Verse that builds AI. People describe the AI assistant or AI app they want, and AIV X asks follow-up questions, writes the AI's instructions and rules, builds the working app, and lets them train it with their own knowledge and deploy it with a share link, a website chat bubble or a single HTML file. The first build is free. AIV X is at aiv-x.com.
