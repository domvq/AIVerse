Medical AI research: EMARI

EMARI, the Emergency Medicine AI Research Initiative, is AI Verse's medical AI research team. It builds AI and machine-learning prototypes for emergency medical services, tests them, writes detailed reports on how each one was built and evaluated, and publishes its prototypes, code and results.

Its work includes AI tools for paramedics and EMS students, ECG reading, field reference and patient-care reporting.

Visit EMARI

How we do research

  • Test before release. No model or major update ships without passing its evaluation sets.
  • Show the method. Results come with the tasks, scoring and dates behind them.
  • Name the limits. Every result lists what the model still gets wrong.
  • Keep people safe. Medical, legal and financial answers are treated as general information, and our tools say so.
