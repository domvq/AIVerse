Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who helps us run the Services, and the choices you have.

Updated November 8, 2026

1. The short version

  • Most of what you put into our apps is saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising. We don't run ad trackers or analytics.
  • When you agree to our Terms, content you send to our AI services, such as AIV X, Ask AIV and AI Business, may be used to improve and train AI Verse's AI models. You can opt out anytime (section 4A).
  • When you ask an AI something, your message is sent to an AI model provider to get the answer.

2. What we collect

  • Account info when you sign in: your name, email or phone number, profile photo and sign-in provider (Google, Apple, phone or email). Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase.
  • Purchase info when you buy: your email, what you bought, and subscription status. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • AIs you build or buy: the name, instructions, files, website text and other information you give the AI, and the contact email you enter at checkout.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form or email, including your name, email and message.
  • Usage limits: to count free daily uses and prevent abuse, our server keeps a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address with a use count. We don't store your raw IP address for this.
  • API and AIV X keys: we store your plan, usage counts and a hashed version of your key. Right after checkout we keep a copy of the new key for up to one hour so we can show it to you, then it's deleted.
  • AIV X sync: if you choose to sync an app with your AIV X account, that app's saved data is stored in your AIV X account so you can use it on other devices.

Conversations and builds: messages, files and instructions you send to AIV X, Ask AIV, AI Business and the AI features of our apps are sent to an AI model provider to produce an answer, and we may keep them to run the Services and, as described in section 4A, to improve and train our AI models.

3. What stays on your device

Our apps save your data (tasks, notes, workouts, meals, body stats, budgets, holdings, resumes, photos and settings, including any API keys you add) in your browser's local storage on your device. It stays there until you clear it, unless you choose to sync it with AIV X. Anyone with access to your device and browser may be able to see it, so use a device you trust. Market-data and other provider keys you add are sent from your browser directly to that provider, not to us.

4. How we use it

  • To improve and train AI Verse's AI models, as described in section 4A.
  • To run the Services: sign you in, answer your questions, build and host your AIs, and sync your data when you ask.
  • To process payments, send receipts, keys and setup emails, and handle refunds and support.
  • To enforce limits, prevent fraud and abuse, keep the Services secure, and follow the law.
  • To reply to messages you send us.

4A. Using your content to train our AI

AI Verse builds its own AI models (such as AIV-1). When you accept our Terms, you allow us to use content you send to our AI services to improve and train those models.

  • What may be used: your messages, prompts and instructions, the AI's replies, and your ratings and feedback, in AIV X, Ask AIV, AI Business and the AI features of our apps.
  • What is never used: the contents of files and photos you upload; your saved memories; chats in AI Health and Fitness; data inside AI apps you build; data you sync from our apps to your AIV X account (such as health, fitness or financial records); payment details, passwords and API keys; requests made through the AIV API; and conversations between a business's AI chatbot and that business's visitors.
  • Under 18: we don't use content from people we know are under 18 to train our models.
  • How we protect it: before training, we try to remove names, contact details and other information that identifies you, and we limit who can access training data.
  • Don't share sensitive information (such as health conditions, financial account numbers or government IDs) in AI conversations you don't want used this way.
  • Opting out: in AIV X, turn off Improve AIV X for everyone in Settings → Memory. For anything else, email team@aiverse.it.com from your account email and ask us to stop using your content for training. We'll stop for content you send after that and confirm by email. Content already used in a model that has finished training can't be removed from it.
  • How long: training copies are kept for up to 3 years, or until you delete your account or ask us to delete them.
  • AIV X details: AIV X has its own Privacy Policy, which describes its training setting and the AI providers it uses.

We never sell your content. Outside AIV X, we don't share it with other companies to train their AI models. In AIV X, while "Improve AIV X for everyone" is on, some requests may go to free AI providers whose free plans may use them to improve their own models, as explained in the AIV X Privacy Policy.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Services, only as needed for that purpose, or when the law requires it:

  • AI model providers (such as Groq and Cloudflare Workers AI) receive your messages, and any files or photos you include, to generate answers. If you choose another AI provider in an app's settings (such as OpenAI, Google or your own local AI), your messages go to that provider instead, under its terms.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server and stores the data described above.
  • Google Firebase handles sign-in and AIV X sync.
  • Stripe processes payments and runs the billing portal.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Google Fonts provides its fonts. Both receive standard web request info such as your IP address.
  • Providers you connect yourself, such as Finnhub, Twelve Data, Spotify, Apple Music, Google, Strava, Dropbox, job sites or image services, receive the requests your browser sends them. See section 5A.

If AI Verse is ever involved in a merger or sale, information may transfer as part of it, subject to this policy.

5A. Connected apps and MCP

Each AI app has a Connections button. It only acts when you choose an action.

  • Links, imports and exports (for example LinkedIn and Indeed job searches, calendar files, bank CSV files, Anki decks and spreadsheets) run in your browser. Files you import stay on your device in the app.
  • Sign-ins (Spotify, Apple Music, Google, Strava and Dropbox) open the service's own sign-in page. We never see your password for those services. The access token they give back is stored only in your browser, and you can remove it with Disconnect. The app only uses the access you approve, and only to do what you ask (for example, save a playlist or import your activities). The service's own privacy policy covers what it does with your data.
  • Strava and Apple Music need our server to complete the sign-in. It passes the sign-in through and does not store your tokens or activity.
  • Your bank: we never ask for bank passwords or connect to your bank. You choose a statement file to import, and it stays in your browser.
  • MCP servers you add receive the requests you run from the app, under that server owner's terms. Any access token you enter is stored only in your browser.
  • The AI Verse MCP server (used from the apps or from other AI apps such as Claude or ChatGPT) processes the tool requests it receives to answer them, in the same way as our free app AI. We keep a daily count per visitor, or a monthly count per AIV key, for limits. We don't store the requests or answers.

6. Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. We use browser storage that's needed for the Services to work: keeping you signed in (Firebase), saving your app data and settings, and remembering choices like hiding a notice. Stripe uses its own cookies on its checkout and billing pages to process payments and prevent fraud.

7. How long we keep it

  • Free-use counters: about 70 days.
  • Account, subscription and key records: while your account or subscription is active, and as long as needed afterward for tax, accounting and legal reasons.
  • AIs you build or buy: until you ask us to delete them or your access ends.
  • Conversations and content kept to run the Services and for training: as long as needed for those purposes, or until you ask us to delete them, except content already used in a trained model.
  • Contact messages: as long as needed to help you.
  • Data in your browser: until you clear it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can clear app data anytime from each app's settings or your browser. You can ask us to access, correct, delete or send you a copy of personal information we hold about you by emailing team@aiverse.it.com. We'll confirm the request comes from you and respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as objecting to or limiting some uses. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

9. California residents

In the past 12 months we have collected the categories described in section 2: identifiers (such as name, email, phone and hashed IP address), commercial information (purchases), and content you provide. We use them for the purposes in sections 4 and 4A and disclose them only to the service providers in section 5. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't knowingly sell or share the information of anyone under 16. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing (which we don't do). Email us to use these rights. You may use an authorized agent.

10. Children

The Services aren't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store API keys in hashed form (apart from the one-hour copy after checkout), and limit who can access our systems. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

12. International users

AI Verse is based in California, United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By using the Services, you understand your information may be processed there.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for important changes, tell account holders by email or on the site.

14. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: team@aiverse.it.com or our contact page.
