AI Verse in one paragraph

AI Verse is a California AI company that builds practical AI tools for everyday life. It offers seven free AI apps for studying, planning, money, fitness, photos, music and careers, a free AI assistant called Ask AIV, a no-code builder for personal AI assistants, and AIV X, the AI platform that builds AI assistants and AI apps from a plain-language description. AI Verse also builds AI chatbots and custom AI tools for businesses, offers the AIV API for developers, and creates its own AI datasets. Its slogan is Build Your AI Future.

What you can do with AI Verse

  • AIV X, our AI platform. Describe an AI assistant or a full AI app and AIV X designs, builds and deploys it, with no code. Your first build is free. AIV X also lives at aiv-x.com.
  • Seven free AI apps, each with 20 free AI uses a day: AI Personal Assistant (personal assistant and planner), AI Study and Research (AI tutor and research lab), AI Stock and Finance (markets, paper trading and budgeting), AI Health and Fitness (workouts and nutrition), AI Photo (photo editing and image creation), AI Music (AI playlists and music discovery) and AI Career (resumes, job search and interviews).
  • Ask AIV, a free AI assistant that answers questions and helps you find the right tool.
  • AI Business, a no-code builder for a personal AI assistant that knows your notes, goals and routine. Try it free.
  • AI for business: an AI chatbot for your website, or a custom AI tool built for your business.
  • The AIV API, an OpenAI-compatible AI API for developers, with one key that also runs every AI Verse app.
  • Research: our AI models (AIV-1, AIV-1 Mini, AIV Code and more), how we test them, and EMARI, our medical AI research team for emergency medical services.
  • Partners: AI Verse is a registered partner of OpenAI.
  • AI Verse Data: research datasets, training data and benchmarks that AI Verse creates itself.

Who it's for

Students, workers, families, creators and small businesses who want AI to help with real tasks without learning to code. Developers use the AIV API and AIV X to put AI into their own products.

How it works

  • Free to start. Every app includes 20 free AI uses a day with no setup. Ask AIV is free, and AI Business and AIV X let you try a build free.
  • Your data stays with you. The apps save your information in your own browser. We don't sell your data. With your agreement to our Terms, content you send to our AI services helps train our own AI models, and you can opt out anytime. See our Privacy Policy.
  • One account. Sign in once and use the same account on AI Verse and AIV X.
  • Bring your own AI if you like. Each app can also run on an AI key of your choice or an AI model on your own computer.
