How to use AI Health and Fitness, a free AI workout and nutrition coach, to train consistently and eat for your goals.

Read this first

AI Health and Fitness gives general fitness and nutrition information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or exercise program, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, have a medical condition or take medication. In an emergency, call 911.

What it does

AI Health and Fitness is a free AI coach with 20 free AI uses a day. It includes:

  • Workout plans built around your goal, experience, schedule and equipment, with automatic progression.
  • A workout logger for sets, reps and weights, with rest timers.
  • Calorie and macro tracking, food lookup, meal photos and AI meal plans with grocery lists.
  • Water and sleep tracking, body measurements, progress photos and posture feedback.
  • An AI coach you can ask about training and nutrition.

Step 1: Set up your profile

Open Settings and enter your sex, age, height and weight, then choose your goal (lose fat, build muscle, recomposition, strength, general fitness or endurance), activity level, experience, training days and equipment. Your targets stay at 0 until you enter your body stats, so nothing is guessed for you.

How your targets are set

  • Calories start from your resting energy use, estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, adjusted for your activity and workouts, then for your goal.
  • Fat loss is capped at a gradual pace of about 1% of body weight per week.
  • Protein is set at about 1.6–2.0 g per kg of body weight, depending on your goal.
  • You can replace any target with your own number, such as one from your doctor or dietitian.

These are estimates. Watch your real progress for two to three weeks and adjust.

Step 2: Train

Open Training to see today's workout. Log each set as you go, and a rest timer runs between sets. The plan tracks your progress and adjusts your weights as you get stronger. Ask the AI to swap an exercise if something hurts or you lack equipment, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.

Step 3: Eat and track

Log meals by searching foods, describing a meal or taking a photo, then check your calories and macros against your targets. Ask for a meal plan that fits your diet and a grocery list for the week.

Step 4: Track progress

Log your weight and measurements weekly, add progress photos, and review trends instead of single days. Weight changes day to day with water and food; the trend over weeks is what matters.

Your privacy

Your workouts, meals, measurements and photos are saved in your own browser, not on our servers. Read the Privacy Policy.
