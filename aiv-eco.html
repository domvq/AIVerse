Why AI uses water

Every AI answer runs on computers in a data center. Those computers get hot, and many data centers cool them by evaporating water. The power plants that supply their electricity use water too. So the water an answer uses depends on how much computing it takes, which model runs it, and where and when it runs.

Published estimateWaterEnergySource

These studies count different things (cooling water only, or power-plant water and manufacturing too), so the figures are not directly comparable. They agree on one point: the water behind an answer can differ by more than 100 times depending on the model and the size of the request.

Our estimate: up to 60–90% less water on certain tasks

We estimate savings by comparing the computing a task needs with and without Eco's limits, using published measurements of how energy changes with answer length, sources read and reasoning passes. Because cooling and power-plant water rise and fall with energy, the same percentages apply to water when the model and data center stay the same.

Basis figures are published per-request energy estimates from Jegham et al. (2025) for widely used AI models. They illustrate how much computing each kind of request needs; they are not measurements of AIV Eco itself.

What these numbers are, and what they are not

  • They are estimates. AI Verse has not yet measured AIV Eco's water use directly, and the figures have not been independently verified.
  • "Up to" means on certain tasks. Savings are largest on research, long reports and deep reasoning. Quick questions save little, because they are already small.
  • Results vary. Actual water use depends on the AI model you choose, the company that runs it, the data center's location and cooling, and the time of day.
  • Shorter is a trade-off. Eco's answers are briefer and use fewer sources by design. When you need a long report or step-by-step reasoning, you can ask for it or launch AIV X.
  • We will publish measurements. As we collect real usage data, we will replace these estimates with measured results and show the method here.

Questions

Do I need my own AI key?

No. AIV Eco works right away on AI Verse's AIV model. If you'd rather use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Groq, add your own key and Eco will point you to where to get one.

How is AIV Eco different from AIV X?

AIV X is our full AI platform, including AI agents and the AI builder. AIV Eco keeps the everyday assistant and leaves those heavier features out, so each request uses as little computing as possible.

Does using less water make the answers worse?

Answers are shorter and draw on fewer sources, which is the point. For most everyday questions that is all you need. For long reports or deep analysis, ask for more detail or use AIV X.

Is the 60–90% figure guaranteed?

No. It is our estimate for certain tasks, based on published research about how AI energy and water use change with the size of a request. Your results depend on the model, the provider and the task.
