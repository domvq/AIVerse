How to build an AI app without code

Describe the AI app or assistant you want, and AIV X, the AI platform from AI Verse, builds it for you.

What AIV X builds

AIV X is a no-code AI app builder. It makes two kinds of AI:

  • AI assistants: chat AIs with their own personality, rules and knowledge, like a study tutor, a writing partner or a support agent for your business.
  • AI apps: working web apps with AI inside, with screens, buttons and inputs, like a flashcard trainer, a meal planner, a story generator or a budgeting tool.

You don't write code. You describe what you want, and AIV X asks questions, writes the AI's identity, instructions, tone and guardrails, and for apps, builds the whole app.

Step 1: Write a good request

The clearer your first message, the better your first build. Cover five things:

  1. Who it's for: "nursing students," "my bakery's customers," "my family."
  2. What it does: the main job, in one sentence.
  3. What people put in: a topic, a photo, a list of ingredients, a budget.
  4. What they get out: a quiz, a plan, an answer, a chart.
  5. Rules and tone: what it must never do and how it should sound.

Example: "Build a flashcard app for nursing students. They type a topic, and it makes 10 flashcards, then quizzes them one card at a time and tracks which ones they missed. Keep explanations short and friendly, and remind them to check facts against their textbook."

Step 2: Let AIV X build it

Send your request on the AIV X page or at aiv-x.com. AIV X may ask a few follow-up questions, then builds your AI. Apps take about a minute, and you'll see a live preview you can use right away. Your first build is free.

Step 3: Refine it by asking

Test it like a real user. When something should change, just say so: "make the buttons bigger," "add a timer to each question," "answer in Spanish." AIV X rebuilds it with your change.

Step 4: Train it with your knowledge

With an AIV X plan, AIV X Studio lets you add what your AI should know: pasted text, files, a website, or example questions with ideal answers. It answers from your knowledge first. Your knowledge is stored on AI Verse and is never put inside your downloads.

Step 5: Deploy it

  • Share link: a hosted page you can send to anyone or open on your phone.
  • Website widget: paste one line before </body> to add a chat bubble to your site.
  • HTML download: one complete HTML file you can host anywhere, such as GitHub Pages or Netlify.
  • Settings file: your AI's full blueprint as JSON, to back up or re-import later.

Deploying and your library of every AI you make are part of an AIV X plan, so you can come back and improve any AI anytime.
