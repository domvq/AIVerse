Setup guide

Set up your AIV key

Step 1

Choose a plan

Go to the API page, pick Starter, Pro or Business, and pay securely through Stripe. AI Verse never sees your card details.

Step 2

Copy your key

After checkout you return to the API page and your key is shown once. It starts with aiv_live_. Save it somewhere safe, like a password manager.

Step 3

Paste it into the app

In any AI Verse app, open Settings → AI provider → AIV key, paste the key, then press Test connection.

Step 4

Use it everywhere

The same key works in every AI Verse app. Check your usage or replace a lost key anytime under My key.

Your key is saved only in your browser and sent only to AI Verse. Keep it private, and replace it from My key if it is ever shared.
