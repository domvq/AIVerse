Setup

Ollama

  1. Install from ollama.com and download a model, e.g. ollama pull qwen3:8b (7–8B models or larger work best for planning and email).
  2. Allow this site to talk to Ollama by setting OLLAMA_ORIGINS to , then restart Ollama.
    Mac: launchctl setenv OLLAMA_ORIGINS "" · Windows: add it under System → Environment Variables · Linux: OLLAMA_ORIGINS= ollama serve

LM Studio

  1. Load a model, open the Developer tab and start the server.
  2. Turn on Enable CORS in the server settings.

If your browser asks to let this site access devices on your local network, allow it.

A small model downloads once into this browser and runs on your graphics card — fully private, and it works offline afterwards. Needs Chrome or Edge with WebGPU. Answers are simpler than cloud models. Song picks from small models are less reliable; cloud models give the best playlists.


Your data

Your playlists, saved songs, taste profile and listening history are stored only in this browser. What you ask the AI goes only to the provider you chose. Song searches go to Apple's free iTunes Search service.


Song details, artwork and 30-second previews are provided by Apple through the iTunes Search API. Listen to full songs on Apple Music, Spotify or YouTube Music.
